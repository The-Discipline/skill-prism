# Prismatize

**One decision, five angles, one verdict.**

A decision council for one-person businesses: freelancers, consultants, coaches, trainers and solo creators. Bring a choice between options. Five advisors answer separately, two reviewers judge their answers anonymously, a chairman gives one verdict and one action for the next 48 hours, and a checker tests the verdict before you see it. When the evidence is missing, Prismatize says "not yet" and names the cheapest way to find out. When the decision belongs to someone else, such as a lawyer or an accountant, it says so.

## Use it

Say "prismatize this:" followed by your decision. In Claude Code, type `/prismatize`. For help, say "prismatize help".

Prismatize is only as good as the context you give it: your offers, prices, clients and available hours. Without them, it gives generic advice.

Prismatize has not been run on a real decision yet.
